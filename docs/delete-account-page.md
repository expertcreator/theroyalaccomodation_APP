This page explains how to delete your account for **The Royal Accommodation** app (iPhone and Android), made by The Royal Accommodation.

### Delete your account in the app

1. Open The Royal Accommodation app and sign in.
2. Tap the menu button (top right), then tap **Settings**.
3. Tap **Delete My Account**.
4. Enter your password and tap **Delete** to confirm.

Your account is deleted straight away.

### Delete your account by email

If you can't use the app, email us at info@theroyalaccommodation.com from the email address on your account, with the subject "Delete my account". We will delete your account within 30 days and confirm by email.

### What is deleted

- Your login (email address and password)
- Your profile details: name, phone number, country and address
- Your profile photo

### What is kept

Records of bookings you have made are not deleted with your account. We keep them for 6 years, as required by law for accounting purposes, and then delete them. See our [Privacy Policy](https://theroyalaccommodation.com/policy/privacy-policy/) for more details.
