## Our Mobile App

This policy also covers The Royal Accommodation app for iPhone and Android. You can browse our residences and check availability without an account. To book or manage your stays in the app you need to create an account.

### What the app collects

- **Account details:** your name, email address and password. Your password is handled by Google Firebase Authentication and is never visible to us.
- **Profile details:** your country, and optionally your phone number, address and profile photo. You can change or remove these at any time in My Profile.
- **Booking details:** your dates, number of guests and pets, and the booking itself. Bookings are stored in our booking system, the same as bookings made on this website.
- **Messages:** if you send us an enquiry from the app, we receive your name, email address, optional phone number and your message.

The app does not use advertising, does not track you across other apps or websites, and does not collect your location.

### Where it is stored

Your account, profile and profile photo are stored securely with Google Firebase, a service we use to run the app. Only you can see your profile photo. We do not sell your information or share it with anyone for marketing.

### Card payments in the app

Card payments in the app are processed by Stripe. You enter your card details into Stripe's secure form, and we never see or store your full card number. When you pay, Stripe keeps your card on file so we can charge it for future payments in line with our Terms & Conditions. This is shown to you before you pay.

### Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time in the app: open the menu, tap **Settings**, then **Delete My Account**, and enter your password to confirm. This permanently deletes your login, your profile details and your profile photo straight away.

If you can't use the app, email us at info@theroyalaccommodation.com from the email address on your account and ask us to delete it. We will do so within 30 days.

Booking records are not deleted with your account. As explained in "Storing Your Data" above, we keep them for 6 years for accounting purposes.
